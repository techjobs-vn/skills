# Vietnam payroll and labour rules for an offer review

Rates checked 2026-11-09. These rates are effective from 2026-07-01, not historical or future periods. Figures change by decree, often on 1 January or 1 July: before using another period, check primary sources.

Scope: ordinary resident payroll for a Vietnamese employee with a labour contract of at least three months, using progressive withholding. Short contracts can have different withholding even when insurance applies. Non-residents, foreign-worker insurance arrangements, freelancers, and special tax cases are outside this method. This is a monthly payroll estimate, not final annual reconciliation.

## Personal income tax on salary

From the 2026 tax period:

- Deductions: 15,500,000/month for the taxpayer, 6,200,000/month per registered dependent ([Quốc hội](https://quochoi.vn/tintuc/Pages/tin-hoat-dong-cua-quoc-hoi.aspx?ItemID=96140)).
- Progressive schedule, 5 brackets, on monthly taxable income ([Cục Thuế TP.HCM](https://tphcm.gdt.gov.vn/wps/wcm/connect/TPHCM/site/news/cucthue/246e1161-c9a4-4020-a9a3-cc7b0b965094?presentationTemplate=Lib/pt_new_detail_print)):

  | Taxable income / month | Rate |
  |---|---|
  | up to 10 million | 5% |
  | 10–30 million | 10% |
  | 30–60 million | 20% |
  | 60–100 million | 30% |
  | above 100 million | 35% |

- Taxable income = gross − employee insurance − deductions. A 13th-month salary or bonus is taxed in the month it is paid, so that month's net is lower than a plain twelfth.

## Compulsory insurance, employee share

- Social insurance 8%, health insurance 1.5%, unemployment insurance 1% of the insurance salary base (employer pays about 21.5% on top; this is not deducted from the employee).
- Caps on the base:
  - social + health: 20 × reference salary = 50,600,000 (reference salary 2,530,000 from 2026-07-01; 46,800,000 before). The new base is confirmed by [the Government's decree announcement](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/tu-01-7-chinh-phu-tang-luong-co-so-119260517100243881.htm).
  - unemployment: 20 × regional minimum wage.
- Regional minimum wage from 2026-01-01 (Nghị định 293/2025/NĐ-CP): region I 5,310,000; II 4,730,000; III 4,140,000; IV 3,700,000. Most of Hà Nội and TP.HCM is region I; the decree's annex lists each ward and commune.
- Primary verification: [Government regional wage table](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/tu-1-1-2026-muc-luong-toi-thieu-duoc-tang-bao-nhieu-119251111091442629.htm), [Government personal/dependent deductions](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/cach-tinh-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-tu-tien-luong-tien-cong-119260623094516526.htm). The PIT source above is the tax authority; its search result confirms the upper brackets, while direct retrieval was unavailable during the 2026-11-09 check. This is not a full statutory audit.
- **Insurance base below the real salary** is common: the employer declares a lower "lương đóng bảo hiểm" and pays the rest as allowances. Net is higher today, but pension, sick and maternity pay, and unemployment benefit are all computed on the declared base. Ask the employer for the figure and use it as the insurance base in the calculation.

## Labour Code 2019 points that come up in offers

- **Probation**: at most 60 days for a role that needs a college degree or higher (180 days for enterprise managers, 30 for technician-level roles); pay at least 85% of the official salary. One probation per role.
- **Resignation notice by the employee**: general rules differ by contract type and exceptions. Identify what the contract asks and verify current rules before quoting a statutory duration. Enforceability is a question for a lawyer.
- **13th-month salary** is not required by law: it is whatever the contract or the company's policy says. If the offer counts it in "annual package", ask whether it is guaranteed or performance-based.
- **Fixed-term renewals**: ask about renewal plans and verify statutory exceptions before giving legal conclusions.
- **Annual leave**: at least 12 working days a year for normal work, plus 1 day per 5 years of service.

## Gross, Net and USD offers

- "Net" offers mean the employer pays tax and insurance on top; check whether the Net figure is guaranteed if tax rules change (they did in 2026).
- USD offers are usually paid in VND at a stated rate or the day's rate; ask which, since exchange rate swings move the net.
